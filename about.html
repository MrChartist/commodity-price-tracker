About this tool

The India Commodity Price Tracker shows what commodities may cost to import into India. It takes international futures prices, converts them to Indian units, applies the live USD/INR rate and adds customs duty.

What it does

  • Covers 33 commodities: precious metals, industrial and battery metals, energy and agri.
  • Shows import landed prices in rupees, in units used in India such as per gram, per kg, per quintal and per tonne.
  • Runs fully in your browser. There is no server, login or database.
  • Explains every formula, duty rate and data source in the documentation.

This is an educational tool. It is not financial advice and it does not use NSE or MCX data. Some commodities use manually updated indicative levels. Read the disclaimer and data limitations.

Who builds it

Mr. Chartist is an India-focused stock market research analyst and educator. This tracker is part of the mrchartist.com ecosystem of free tools for the Indian market community.
