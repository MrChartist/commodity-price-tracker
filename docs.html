How the India Commodity Price Tracker calculates import landed prices for 33 commodities, which data it uses, and where its limits are. Every duty rate, unit and source on this page matches the configuration in app.js.

Overview

The tracker is a free, client-side web page. It takes international futures prices, converts them to Indian units, multiplies by the USD/INR rate and adds the customs duty. The result is an estimate of the import landed price in rupees.

Gold, Silver, Platinum, Palladium.

Pricing formula

Every commodity follows the same four steps.

Master formulaIndia landed ₹ per unit = (International price ÷ conversion divisor) × USD/INR × (1 + duty rate)
Duty rateDuty rate = the total shown in the duty matrix (BCD + AIDC + SWS, as applicable)
  1. Get the international priceYahoo Finance futures price in USD for 24 commodities, or a manually updated indicative level for 9 commodities. Quotes in US cents or other currencies are first converted to US dollars.
  2. Convert the unitThe price is divided by the conversion divisor of the commodity (see Unit conversions) to get a price per Indian unit.
  3. Apply USD/INRThe rate comes from the ExchangeRate API (open.er-api.com). If that fails, Yahoo Finance USDINR=X is used.
  4. Add customs dutyThe commodity-specific duty is added. Duty can depend on the date (cotton) and Live Cattle has no landed price because import is prohibited.

The dashboard refreshes about every 60 seconds while the tab is visible. Polling pauses in hidden tabs and refreshes when you return.

Data sources

The tracker has no server of its own. The browser fetches data directly from public services.

Primary

Yahoo Finance

Futures prices for 24 commodities and the USD/INR fallback. Reached through public CORS proxies (allorigins.win, corsproxy.io, codetabs.com).

FX

ExchangeRate API

open.er-api.com gives USD/INR and the other rates used for non-USD quotes and the FX strip.

Backup

gold-api.com

Spot price for Gold, Silver, Platinum and Palladium when Yahoo Finance fails. No change data is available from it.

Manual

Indicative levels

Zinc, Nickel, Lead, Tin, Iron Ore, Lithium (Carbonate), Cobalt, Canola Oil, Palm Oil (CPO). Updated by hand in app.js. Last update: 2026-06-12.

Charts

Lightweight Charts

Open-source charting library by TradingView (v4.1.3). Chart history comes from Yahoo Finance for the commodities that have a futures symbol.

Browser

localStorage cache

Last prices and FX are saved in your browser. Chart series are cached for 6 hours.

Data freshness and limitations

  • Refresh. The page refreshes about every 60 seconds. Data older than 10 minutes is marked STALE. If a refresh fails, the last saved prices are shown and marked CACHED or STALE.
  • Futures, not spot. Yahoo Finance gives front-month futures prices, which can differ from spot and from Indian market rates. Quotes may be delayed.
  • Indicative levels. 9 commodities do not change live. They show a ~INDICATIVE badge and were last updated on 2026-06-12. Their prices and daily change are not live.
  • Proxies and rate limits. Free public proxies and APIs can be slow, blocked or rate-limited. A commodity may show "No data" for a while.
  • Charts. Charts are available only for commodities with a futures symbol. Indicative commodities have no chart.
  • Duty only. The landed price includes customs duty only. It excludes GST, freight, insurance, port and handling charges, local premiums, quality differences and dealer margins.
  • Duty accuracy. Duty rates are entered manually and may lag official changes. Needs verification before any decision.
  • Steel. HRC=F is a US domestic price and is normally much higher than global or Indian ex-mill levels.
  • Livestock. Lean hog futures track live hogs, not retail pork. Live Cattle shows the international price only.

Glossary

34 terms
Landed price
The estimated cost of bringing a commodity into India. The tracker adds the converted international price, the USD/INR rate and the customs duty. It excludes freight, insurance, GST, port charges and dealer margins.
BCD (Basic Customs Duty)
The main import duty charged on goods entering India. The rate differs by commodity. See the duty matrix for the rate used by the tracker.
AIDC
Agriculture Infrastructure and Development Cess. An additional levy charged along with BCD on selected goods, for example gold, silver and some edible oils.
SWS (Social Welfare Surcharge)
A surcharge calculated on the BCD amount, not on the value of goods. A 5% BCD with SWS gives about 5.5% in total, as shown in the app labels.
Safeguard duty
A temporary extra duty meant to protect domestic industry from a sudden rise in imports. The steel rate in the app (about 19.75%) includes an allowance for it. Whether it applies depends on conditions. Needs verification against current notifications.
Critical minerals exemption
A customs policy under which selected minerals are imported without BCD. The app uses 0% for tin, lithium and cobalt. Needs verification against the current notification.
TRQ (Tariff Rate Quota)
A system where a fixed quantity of imports is allowed at a lower duty and anything above it pays the higher duty. The app shows corn at 50% and notes a 15% in-quota rate.
DGFT
Directorate General of Foreign Trade. It controls import and export policy. Live cattle and beef imports are prohibited, so the tracker does not show a landed price for them.
USD/INR
The number of Indian rupees for one US dollar. Every landed price is multiplied by this rate, so a small change in USD/INR moves all prices.
Front-month futures
The futures contract nearest to expiry. Yahoo Finance symbols ending in =F, such as GC=F, usually refer to this contract. It is not the same as the spot price.
Spot price
The price for immediate delivery. For precious metals the tracker uses gold-api.com spot only as a backup when Yahoo Finance fails.
Indicative price
A manually updated reference level used where no free live feed exists. The cards show a ~INDICATIVE badge. It does not change by itself and can be out of date.
COMEX
Commodity Exchange, part of CME Group. Source of gold, silver and copper futures (GC=F, SI=F, HG=F).
NYMEX
New York Mercantile Exchange, part of CME Group. Source of WTI crude oil, natural gas, platinum and palladium futures.
CME
Chicago Mercantile Exchange. Source of aluminium, steel HRC, lumber, lean hogs and live cattle futures used here.
CBOT
Chicago Board of Trade, part of CME Group. Source of wheat, corn, soybean, soybean oil, soybean meal and rough rice futures.
ICE
Intercontinental Exchange. Source of Brent crude, sugar, cotton, coffee, cocoa and orange juice futures.
LME
London Metal Exchange. The global reference market for base metals. Yahoo Finance has no usable LME feed, so zinc, nickel, lead and tin use indicative levels.
US cents (USX)
Many US grain, softs and livestock contracts are quoted in cents. Yahoo Finance flags these as USX. The tracker divides by 100 to get US dollars first.
Troy ounce
The weight unit for precious metals. 1 troy ounce is 31.1035 grams in the app, which is used as the divisor.
Metric tonne
1,000 kg. Used for most base metals and for cocoa and oils.
Short ton
The US ton of 2,000 lb, equal to 907.185 kg. Steel HRC and soybean meal futures are quoted per short ton.
Pound (lb)
0.453592 kg. Used for copper, sugar, cotton, coffee, soybean oil, orange juice and livestock futures.
Bushel
A volume-based grain unit. The app uses 60 lb (27.2155 kg) for wheat and soybean, and 56 lb (25.4012 kg) for corn.
Hundredweight (cwt)
A US weight of 100 lb, equal to 45.3592 kg. Rough rice futures are quoted per cwt.
Quintal
100 kg. The usual mandi unit for grains in India.
Candy
An Indian cotton trade unit. The app uses 356 kg per candy for the secondary cotton price.
Board foot
A lumber volume unit. Lumber futures are for 1,000 board feet, which the app converts to 83.33 cubic feet.
MMBtu
One million British thermal units. The standard unit for natural gas prices.
Karat (K)
Gold purity on a scale of 24. The app shows 24K, 22K (24K × 22/24) and 18K (24K × 18/24).
Fineness
Silver purity in parts per thousand. The app shows 999, 925 (sterling) and 900 (coin).
Contract equivalent
The per-gram or per-kg landed price multiplied by a lot size, for example 100 g for Gold Mini. It is a calculation and not an NSE or MCX quote.
Current account deficit (CAD)
The gap when India pays more for imports than it earns from exports. High gold imports add to it, which is one reason for high bullion duties.
Windfall tax
A special levy on oil producers when prices rise sharply. The tracker does not model it. Crude oil duty in the app is fixed at about 0%.
